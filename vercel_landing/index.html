What BlindSpot Does
🚗
Smart Obstacle Detection
Detects people, vehicles, cyclists, and other obstacles in uploaded images or videos using YOLOv8n. Spatial zone analysis (Left / Centre / Right) generates precise spoken warnings.
YOLOv8n · COCO Pretrained
🦓
Zebra Crossing Recognition
Identifies pedestrian crossings using a MobileNetV3-Small classifier fine-tuned on 600 real-world crosswalk images across 4 perspective classes.
MobileNetV3 · Transfer Learning
🔊
Voice Assistance
Generates natural spoken alerts like "Person detected on your right" and "Possible zebra crossing ahead." Cooldown prevents repeated alerts per frame.
gTTS · pyttsx3
🧠
Multimodal Integration
Decision-level fusion of obstacle detection and crossing recognition. Combines outputs into unified priority alerts without a heavy fusion network.
Decision Fusion · Multimodal
🖼️
Image Processing Pipeline
Full DIP pipeline: Gaussian filtering, Otsu & Adaptive thresholding, Canny edge detection, morphological operations, and contour analysis.
OpenCV · DIP
📊
Evaluation & Metrics
Genuine confusion matrix, precision/recall/F1 per class, and training curves from the crosswalk classifier — not fabricated numbers.
scikit-learn · matplotlib
